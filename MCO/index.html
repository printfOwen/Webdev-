Why It Matters

Highlights of the Movie

Academy Award Winner

Won Best Animated Feature at the 75th Academy Awards (2003), the first anime to do so.

Golden Bear

Shared the top prize at the 2002 Berlin International Film Festival.

Box Office Record

Became the highest-grossing film in Japanese history and held the title for almost two decades.

Joe Hisaishi’s Score

An unforgettable orchestral score, paired with the gentle theme song “Always With Me”.

Hand-Drawn Wonder

A richly detailed spirit world created with traditional hand-drawn animation.

Rooted in Folklore

Draws on Japanese spirits (kami), bathhouse culture and the power of a person’s name.

The Story

Synopsis

Ten-year-old Chihiro is unhappy about moving to a new town. On the way, her father takes a wrong turn and the family finds a strange tunnel that leads to what looks like an abandoned amusement park. While her parents help themselves to food at an empty stall, Chihiro explores and meets a mysterious boy named Haku, who tells her to leave before dark.

She is too late. As night falls, the streets fill with spirits, her parents have turned into pigs, and she cannot go home. To survive, Chihiro must get a job at the bathhouse run by the witch Yubaba, who takes her name and renames her Sen.

Working alongside the boiler man Kamaji and the sharp-tongued Lin, Chihiro serves the bathhouse’s strange guests, from a filthy river spirit to the lonely, hungry No-Face. As she grows braver, she sets out to help Haku, free her parents, and remember who she really is.
